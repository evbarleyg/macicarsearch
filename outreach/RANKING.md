## 0. The arithmetic every dealer here uses

Every WA quote on the board resolves to the same formula, verified against four
separate F&I worksheets:

```
OTD = selling x 1.108  +  doc fee  +  license/government fees
```

Tax is 10.8% and is charged on the **selling price only** — none of these four dealers
taxed the doc fee, even though they could. Doc fee is $200 at both stores and both have
said it does not move. License is an estimate: $500 Kirkland, $650 Puyallup on the two
2021/2023 Carbons, $750 Puyallup on the Turbo.

Practical consequence, and the single most useful number to carry into a showroom:

> **Every $1 off the selling price is $1.108 off the out-the-door.**
> **Every $100 you want off the OTD requires $90.25 off the selling price.**

So when a salesperson answers an out-the-door ask with a monthly payment or a
selling-price move, you can convert instantly in your head and tell them whether it
actually got there.

  price is settled.

  can be finalized at a desk she is not sitting at.
- No acceptance by email, ever. Commitment happens in person or by phone.
