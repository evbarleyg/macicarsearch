### v3 message standard (Evan's directive, 2026-08-11 PM — use in every reply and any future form)

Every dealer message leads with these three points, in this order:

1. **Email is our contact method — please don't call.** Replies by email only; a phone call will not move the process forward.
2. **We are narrowing 10 cars down to 3 this week.** The way a car makes the top 3 is a written, itemized **best out-the-door price** (selling price, doc fee, tax rate, license/title/RTA, any add-ons on their own lines marked optional) plus the Carfax/AutoCheck.
3. **Test drives come after the number, not before.** We'll schedule test drives only for the finalists — polite but explicit: "please don't reply asking us to come in and drive it first; the OTD email is the step that gets you a visit."

Copy-paste reply for any dealer who calls instead of emailing:

```text
Thanks for reaching out. Quick heads-up on how we're buying: we're comparing 10 CX-5s and narrowing to 3 this week, and we're doing it entirely over email. Could you reply here with your best itemized out-the-door price (selling price, doc fee, tax rate, license/title/RTA, any add-ons listed separately) plus the Carfax/AutoCheck? The written OTD is what gets a car into our top 3 — we'll schedule test drives for the finalists only. Email only please, no calls. Thanks! — Evan
```

### Fees — legitimate vs strike
**Legitimate:**
- Selling price (must equal or beat the advertised price — WA WAC 308-66-152(4)(j),(k); OR OAR 137-020-0020(3)).
- Sales tax (WA dealer: at the dealer's city vehicle rate) or WA use tax (OR dealer: at your Seattle rate) — ask them to state the rate/location code used.
- Actual state title/registration/plate fees + Sound Transit RTA excise (1.1% × MSRP × depreciation) + Seattle $50 TBD — roughly $200–350 fixed + $200–350 RTA.
- Doc fee: WA capped at $200 and must be disclosed as negotiable ([RCW 46.70.180](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=46.70.180)); OR capped at $250 with an e-filing integrator / $200 without, negotiable ([ORS 822.043](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_822.043)). Not taxed in WA.
- OR only: $35 21-day trip permit if the dealer does not file WA plates.

**Strike / refuse:**
- "Reconditioning", "certification", "prep", "inspection", "dealer services" fees.
- Nitrogen, VIN etch, paint/fabric/theft "protection packages", door-edge/appearance packages, GPS/LoJack, "market adjustment".
- Oregon "CAT" / "corporate activity tax recovery" shown as a tax — it is a dealer pass-through, negotiable price (Oregon DOR).
- Oregon 0.5% "vehicle privilege/use tax" — new vehicles only; should not appear on a used CX-5.
- Any F&I product bundled into the OTD (service contract, GAP, maintenance plan, key/tire-and-wheel) unless you asked for it.
- If they call a package mandatory: "I don't need [it]; since the advertised price has to stand on its own, please quote the car without it or net it out of the price." ([WAC 308-66-152](https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=308-66-152) · [WA AG dealer advertising](https://www.atg.wa.gov/dealer-advertising) · [OAR 137-020-0020](https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_137-020-0020))

### Never say (in any email or at the desk)
- A target price or "what would it take" in round 1 — let them bid first.
- Your credit-union rate, a monthly payment, a down-payment amount, or "how will you be paying" details before the OTD is fixed ("I'm focused only on the total out-the-door price").
- A phone number (forms will demand one — skip the form rather than give it).
- Other dealers' names or exact numbers (say "a comparable CX-5 is available to us at $X"; show proof only if asked and only after your counter).
- Legal citations in round 1; threats; "final offer" language you don't mean; more than one counter plus one half-step.
- Anything about the report, the website, days-on-lot counts, or how the shortlist was built.
- "We'll finalize when we get there" — never agree to re-open numbers in the showroom.
