Fees — legitimate

  • Selling price (must equal or beat the advertised price — WA WAC 308-66-152(4)(j),(k); OR OAR 137-020-0020(3)).
  • Sales tax (WA dealer: at the dealer's city vehicle rate) or WA use tax (OR dealer: at your Seattle rate) — ask them to state the rate/location code used.
  • Actual state title/registration/plate fees + Sound Transit RTA excise (1.1% × MSRP × depreciation) + Seattle $50 TBD — roughly $200–350 fixed + $200–350 RTA.
  • Doc fee: WA capped at $200 and must be disclosed as negotiable (RCW 46.70.180); OR capped at $250 with an e-filing integrator / $200 without, negotiable (ORS 822.043). Not taxed in WA.
  • OR only: $35 21-day trip permit if the dealer does not file WA plates.

Fees — strike / refuse

  • "Reconditioning", "certification", "prep", "inspection", "dealer services" fees.
  • Nitrogen, VIN etch, paint/fabric/theft "protection packages", door-edge/appearance packages, GPS/LoJack, "market adjustment".
  • Oregon "CAT" / "corporate activity tax recovery" shown as a tax — it is a dealer pass-through, negotiable price (Oregon DOR).
  • Oregon 0.5% "vehicle privilege/use tax" — new vehicles only; should not appear on a used CX-5.
  • Any F&I product bundled into the OTD (service contract, GAP, maintenance plan, key/tire-and-wheel) unless you asked for it.
  • If they call a package mandatory: "I don't need [it]; since the advertised price has to stand on its own, please quote the car without it or net it out of the price." (WAC 308-66-152 · WA AG dealer advertising · OAR 137-020-0020)

Buying from the Oregon dealers (#4, #8)

  • Oregon has no sales tax; as a WA resident you owe WA use tax on the purchase price at your Seattle rate (11.05% in Q3 2026) when the car is titled in WA. DOR: vehicles brought into WA
  • Many Portland-metro franchise stores collect WA use tax and file WA title/plates for you — the round-1 email asks each OR dealer which they do. If they do: their OTD should look like a WA deal (tax 11.05%, WA fees, OR doc ≤ $250).
  • If they don't: you pay them selling price + doc only, leave on a $35 / 21-day Oregon trip permit (ODOT trip permits), and within the permit window visit a King County licensing office with the title paperwork/bill of sale to pay use tax + title/plates/RTA (DOL use tax). Budget the same total either way; new WA plates add ~$50.
  • A used CX-5 should carry no Oregon privilege tax; a "CAT recovery" line is negotiable price, not tax (Oregon DOR CAT). Oregon doc cap: Oregon DOJ — buying a vehicle.

Never say

  • A target price or "what would it take" in round 1 — let them bid first.
  • Your credit-union rate, a monthly payment, a down-payment amount, or "how will you be paying" details before the OTD is fixed ("I'm focused only on the total out-the-door price").
  • A phone number (forms will demand one — skip the form rather than give it).
  • Other dealers' names or exact numbers (say "a comparable CX-5 is available to us at $X"; show proof only if asked and only after your counter).
  • Legal citations in round 1; threats; "final offer" language you don't mean; more than one counter plus one half-step.
  • Anything about the report, the website, days-on-lot counts, or how the shortlist was built.
  • "We'll finalize when we get there" — never agree to re-open numbers in the showroom.

Saturday signing checklist

  • Bring: printed buyer's orders / OTD emails per car, pre-approval letter, driver's licenses, insurance card/binder, a card for the deposit and CU draft/cashier's-check plan for the balance, phone charger.
  • Order: best written deal first; appointments ~90 min apart; tell each store the arrival time you committed to in the counter.
  • At the car: VIN on dash/door matches the paperwork; odometer within ~100 mi of the quote; two keys/fobs; tire tread and brand match; no warning lights; cold start; A/C; CarPlay; all windows/hatch; look for overspray/panel gaps; test drive incl. highway.
  • CPO (#7): get the signed 160-point inspection sheet and the CPO warranty registration showing in-service date; confirm 12-mo/12k limited + 7-yr/100k powertrain from in-service, $0 deductible (Mazda CPO).
  • At the desk: the buyer's order must match the emailed OTD line-for-line. Any new line (recon, package, "required" financing) → decline once; if it stays, thank them and leave for the next appointment.
  • F&I: decline every product by default (service contract, GAP, maintenance, etch, key, tire/wheel). Take dealer financing only if the APR beats the CU at the identical price with nothing attached and no prepayment penalty; otherwise use the pre-approval.
  • Oregon store without WA processing: leave with title/MCO paperwork, bill of sale, odometer statement and the trip permit; book King County licensing within 21 days; budget 11.05% use tax + ~$500–750 fees.
  • Before signing: re-read the numbers box (cash price, doc, tax, license, total), confirm no add-on survived, confirm "we owe" slip lists anything promised (second key, mats, touch-up).
