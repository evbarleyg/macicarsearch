## THE BIG ANSWER: does the 2023 Turbo (YW29065) have the Premium Plus interior set?

**YES. Confirmed from the original Monroney window sticker** (`window_sticker_YW29065_2023_turbo.pdf`, full text in the .txt). Total MSRP **$38,225** ($36,850 base + $1,375 destination, **no individual options**, so this content is all standard on the 25T trim). Originally sold by Mazda of Claremont, CA.

Interior content printed on the sticker:
- **VENTILATED FRONT SEATS**
- **HEATED FRONT & REAR SEATS**
- **HEATED STEERING WHEEL**
- **ACTIVE DRIVING DISPLAY** (head-up display)
- **WIRELESS PHONE CHARGER**
- **AUTO FOLD BLACK DOOR MIRRORS** (power folding)
- DRIVER SEAT MEMORY, 8-way power driver w/ lumbar, POWER PASSENGER SEAT
- LEATHER-TRIMMED SEATS
- BOSE AM/FM/HD/SAT 10-SPEAKER
- 10.25" COLOR DISPLAY, Android Auto / Apple CarPlay, 4 USB
- Dual-zone auto A/C with rear vents, 40/20/40 split rear seat
- Auto-dim rearview mirror w/ HomeLink

Exterior/mechanical highlights: power moonroof, 19-inch black alloys, LED headlights with adaptive front-lighting and high beam control, rain-sensing wipers, windshield wiper de-icer, heated power mirrors, power rear liftgate, rear privacy glass, body-colored roof spoiler, i-ACTIV AWD, Mi-Drive sport/off-road, G-Vectoring Control Plus, Mazda Radar Cruise Control with Stop & Go.

So this car is effectively the loaded turbo: it has every comfort item that was previously being chased on the Kirkland GT Premium, plus the 256 hp engine.

**Horsepower note:** the sticker's TRIM box misprints "227-hp 2.0L DOHC" while its own engine section says "SKYACTIV-G 2.5L TURBO / 227 HORSEPOWER; 310 LB-FT" and the dealer site says 256 hp / 320 lb-ft. Mazda dual-rates this engine: 227 hp on 87 octane, 256 hp on 93 octane. Both figures are correct for the same car depending on fuel.
