Warranty reality check for 2024s: factory cover runs from the in-service date, which no feed exposes. Early-build 2024s (VIN serial ~R03xxxxx, e.g. the BMW-Bellevue and PSX Turbo Premiums) went on sale summer–fall 2023, so their 36-month bumper-to-bumper can end as early as **fall 2026**; late builds (R05xxxxx, e.g. VW McMinnville, Cortes) run to late 2027. **2020 — no, unless the budget drops.** The 2021 refresh is the one that matters for daily use: 2021+ cars have the 10.25-inch Mazda Connect screen and new infotainment stack (2020 has the old 7/8-inch unit and older UI), plus 2021 added standard rear USB/safety tweaks. Every 2020 is now past its 36/36 B2B and at or past the 60-month powertrain warranty (in-service 2019–20 → expired by mid-2025/2026), so there is no factory cover left to differentiate cars, and asking prices run only ~$1.5–2.5k under equivalent 2021s.
