Where the market is soft right now

  • 2023 2.5 Turbo is the standout buy: $28,199 vs Seattle FPP $30,980, ~$2.8k under. Only 2 in the dataset and both under book. If you want the turbo, 2023 is cheaper relative to value than 2022 ($27,300 vs $27,370 FPP = at-market).
  • 2021 GT Reserve at $24,999 is turbo + vented leather + HUD for Grand-Touring money: $2,771 under its own FPP and only $200 more than a 2021 GT.
  • Premium Plus is worth the stretch over Premium: 2023 Premium lowest is $27,545, Premium Plus lowest is $28,915; the $1,370 gap buys 360° camera, vented seats, wireless CarPlay, rear heated seats. That's ~$27/mo.
  • Avoid 2021 Signature: trading $1,400 over Seattle FPP; the GT Reserve one rung down is $1,500 cheaper for the same drivetrain.

Filter & ranking rules

  • Disqualify: any reported accident, fleet / rental prior use, lemon or frame-damage flag, fewer than 6 photos (where photo count is known), missing VIN (unless Craigslist / iSeeCars-flagged), missing price or miles.
  • Trim: unwanted trims (Sport, Select, base 2.5 S) dropped unless priced more than $3k under the year average.
  • Rank: deal rating / KBB FPP delta → $ below market → 1-owner → CPO → miles per dollar; at least 3 non-CarGurus sources and all 3 model years enforced on the shortlist.

NHTSA recalls: 0 open campaigns for MY2021–2023 CX-5 (checked 8/7/26). Verify by VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before purchase.
