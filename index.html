WA-dealer sales are taxed at the dealer's city rate (DOR Q3-2026 combined rate + 0.5% motor-vehicle tax: 9.1–11.2% around Puget Sound), so a Kirkland or Puyallup dealer runs ~0.25 pt under Seattle and Burlington nearly 2 pt under; Oregon-dealer purchases owe 11.05% WA use tax at registration. Tax is applied to the vehicle price; the doc fee and licence estimate are added untaxed, which is how the written dealer sheets received so far are built. Earlier versions of this page used a flat 10.35%, which understated OTD by roughly $200–250.

Filter & ranking rules

  • Disqualify: any reported accident, fleet / rental prior use, lemon or frame-damage flag, fewer than 6 photos (where photo count is known), missing VIN (unless Craigslist / iSeeCars-flagged), missing price or miles.
  • Trim: unwanted trims (Sport, Select, base 2.5 S) dropped unless priced more than $3k under the year average.
  • Rank: deal rating / KBB FPP delta → $ below market → 1-owner → CPO → miles per dollar; at least 3 non-CarGurus sources and all 3 model years enforced on the shortlist.
