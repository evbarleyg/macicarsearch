How we judge a listing

Same rules as the numbered board, so every answer below reads the same way.

  • Right car, right trim: 2021–2023 Mazda CX-5 (a 2024 gets a look if the price is right). Comfort trims first: Premium / Premium Plus, Grand Touring with the Premium Package, Carbon Edition, Turbo, Signature. Base trims (Sport, Select, plain 2.5 S) only count if they are more than $3,000 under the going rate.
  • Under 50,000 miles: and ideally one owner. Higher-mile cars can still be useful as a price benchmark, but they do not go on the board.
  • Clean history: no reported accidents, no rental / fleet past, no lemon or frame-damage flags, a real VIN and at least a handful of real photos. Mazda Certified Pre-Owned is a plus, not a must.
  • Priced at or under market: we check the asking price against KBB Fair Purchase Price and the CarGurus market value for that exact car, look at how long it has been listed and whether the price has been cut, and compare it with the cars already on the board.
  • What it really costs: estimated out-the-door = price × (1 + the dealer city’s sales-tax rate, 9.1–11.2% around here) + $200 doc fee + $600 licence, then a monthly payment at 6.30% APR over 60 months (about $19.47 per $1,000 financed) at $0 and $5,000 down. Dealer-quoted figures never appear on this page.

Details: why, flags, links

Why

  • Fails the mileage rule: 68,769 mi vs the 50,000 cap. The factory powertrain warranty (5 yr / 60k) is already gone, so any repair is out of pocket from day one; every car on the board still has 10–30k mi of powertrain coverage left.
  • Priced at market, not below it: KBB's fair purchase price for this exact car and mileage is $23,390, so $22,970 is only about $420 under; CarGurus calls it a Good Deal at $635 under its market value. The big gap to the board's 2021 GT numbers is almost entirely the extra miles.
  • Comparables say the same: CarMax has a 39k-mile 2021 GT for about $1,000 more (no-haggle), and #5 on the board (Kirkland, 2021 GT with the same Premium Package, 33k mi) is roughly $2,400 more on estimated OTD for half the miles and warranty remaining. Other 60–75k-mile 2021 GTs around Puget Sound list at $22.6k–$24.7k, so this one is mid-pack for its band.
  • The listing itself is clean: sale price $22,970 (was $23,780; two cuts in three weeks, 23 days listed), $200 doc fee disclosed as negotiable and at the WA cap, no add-on packages, 31 real photos, franchise dealer in Seattle proper (11.05% rate, so no tax edge).
  • History is claims-only so far: dealer says CARFAX 1-Owner / clean / local trade and CarGurus + KBB previews agree (1 owner, 0 accidents, not fleet or rental), but the full report would not load read-only. Registration state, service records and last odometer are unverified until someone sees the Carfax PDF.

Flags

  • RED: 68,769 miles — fails the under-50k rule; factory powertrain warranty expired.
  • YELLOW: history depth unverified (full Carfax not viewable read-only; 1-owner / clean rests on dealer claims + AutoCheck preview). Ask for the Carfax PDF: WA-only registration, personal use, service cadence.
  • YELLOW: 'LOW MILES' in the dealer blurb is boilerplate (it is high for a 2021); dealer CarGurus rating 3.3 stars on 3 reviews; 'offers expire daily' language and a 3% card surcharge.
  • GREEN: GT Premium Package (ventilated seats, HUD, heated wheel and rear seats), Soul Red, 1-owner / 0 accidents claimed, no add-ons, doc fee at cap and disclosed, 23 days + two cuts = some leverage, no open recalls for the 2021 CX-5.

Where it would slot vs. the board

By estimated OTD ($26,308, $512/mo) it would sit first, about $1,000 under #10 Columbia 2023 Preferred ($27,346 est.) — but only because it carries roughly twice the miles of anything on the board. Not added; benchmark note only.

Skip.

Details: why, flags, links

Why

  • Fails the trim rule: Select is the 2024 base tier — leatherette, heated front seats, wireless CarPlay, radar cruise, but no ventilated seats, no Bose, no HUD, no moonroof, no power liftgate, no leather. The board only admits a Select at $3,000+ under market, and this one is over it.
  • Priced over book: $26,491 is $1,321 over KBB's fair purchase price for this exact car ($25,170, range $23,170–$27,170) and $2,691 over KBB's national 2024 Select figure ($23,800); the asking price equals KBB's number for a 2024 Premium Plus ($26,500). CarGurus disagrees and calls it a Good Deal at $651 under its market value — the outlier signal, with 0 saves.
  • Sticky pricing: 31–32 days listed with a net change of about −$110 (it went up $200 before coming down $110). Local 2024 Selects undercut it: Rairdon Monroe $24,090 / 35k mi (CarGurus Great Deal), West Hills Bremerton $27,299 with only 14.8k mi; a 2024 Carbon Edition with leather and Bose in Burlington lists at $25,499.
  • Against the board at ~$30.3k est. OTD: same money as #12 Lee Johnson 2023 Preferred with Mazda CPO ($30,059), #20 / #8 Royal Moore 2023 Preferreds with 17k mi ($30,278 / $30,472) — all one tier above Select — and only ~$1.6k under #1 Puyallup 2023 Turbo ($31,823) and the CarMax Premium benchmark #15 ($31,878).
  • What the 2024 actually buys over the board's 2023s: one model year, roughly a year / 12k mi of remaining bumper-to-bumper warranty (powertrain to ~2029), wireless CarPlay and Soul Red. Worth a few hundred dollars, not a $2.7k premium over Select book. 'Ford Blue Certified' is a 90-day / 4,000-mi dealer wrapper with a 14-day return, not Mazda CPO.

Flags

  • RED (rule): Select trim with none of the comfort items; the playbook only admits a Select at $3k+ under market and this one is about $1.3k over KBB's figure for the car.
  • YELLOW: pricing sticky and optimistic — a month listed, net −$110; +$1.3k vs KBB for this VIN, +$2.7k vs the national Select figure; CarGurus 'Good Deal / Hot seller' with 0 saves is the outlier.
  • YELLOW: history depth unverified — full Carfax would not load read-only; 1-owner / 0 accidents rest on AutoCheck + the Carfax 1-Owner badge; rental / lease use, registration state and Mazda in-service date unknown (many used 2024s are ex-rental or ex-loaner; CarGurus says not fleet / rental here, but get the PDF).
  • YELLOW: off-brand unit at a Ford store rated 2.75 stars on CarGurus (12 reviews); Lynnwood's 11.2% is the highest rate on the board (no tax edge).
  • GREEN: clean, unconditional price stack (price + $200 doc, no add-ons, no finance or trade conditions); 32 photos + video; Soul Red; factory bumper-to-bumper still alive and powertrain to ~2029; 14-day / 1,000-mi return; no open recalls for the 2024 CX-5.

Where it would slot vs. the board

8th of 17 by estimated OTD ($30,258, $589/mo): between #12 Lee Johnson 2023 Preferred CPO ($30,059) and #20 Royal Moore 2023 Preferred 17k mi ($30,278) — 2023-Preferred money for a 2024 Select. By value it sits in the bottom third of the board. Not added; kept as a 'what a 2024 Select costs locally' benchmark alongside Rairdon Monroe's $24,090 unit.
